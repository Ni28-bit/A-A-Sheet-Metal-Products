Since 1976

The Shop on Carneal Street

A&A Sheet Metal Products has been making metal do what customers need for almost fifty years. The Lyon family opened the shop in 1976 and passed it down to their sons, Mark and Joe, who kept the brakes bending and the customers coming back for decades.

Today the shop belongs to us — Adam and Zach — two HVAC guys with ten years each in the trade. We bought this business because we believe in what it has always stood for: measure it right, build it right, and stand behind it.
